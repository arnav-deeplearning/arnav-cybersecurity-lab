Resources

Where I actually learn this stuff, and where I pulled context from when building the apps and articles on this site. Every link below was checked by hand -- no guessed or made-up URLs.

Official & Government

CISA / NICCS — Cybersecurity for Students

A page CISA (the federal Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency) built specifically for students -- career profiles, scholarship listings, competitions, and how to start building a cybersecurity path in high school.

https://niccs.cisa.gov/audience/cybersecurity-students ↗
NIST Cybersecurity Framework

The official home of NIST's Cybersecurity Framework (CSF 2.0) -- the government-developed model (Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) that real organizations use to structure their security programs.

https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework ↗

Standards & Frameworks

OWASP Top 10

The industry-standard list of the most critical web application security risks, maintained by the nonprofit OWASP Foundation. Essential vocabulary for understanding how real apps actually get exploited.

https://owasp.org/Top10/ ↗
MITRE ATT&CK

A free, globally used knowledge base cataloging real-world attacker tactics and techniques (like phishing or credential dumping). Professional analysts use this exact framework -- it's a great way to see how attacks get classified in practice.

https://attack.mitre.org/ ↗

Education Nonprofits

National Cybersecurity Alliance (StaySafeOnline)

A nonprofit that co-leads Cybersecurity Awareness Month with CISA, offering free articles, videos, and toolkits on personal cyber hygiene written for a general audience.

https://www.staysafeonline.org/ ↗
(ISC)² Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)

An entry-level credential -- with free self-study resources -- from the nonprofit behind the CISSP. Explicitly designed for people with no prior experience, including students, making it a realistic near-term goal.

https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cc ↗

News & Journalism

Krebs on Security

Brian Krebs' independent, investigative security journalism -- 16+ years of breaking real cybercrime and data-breach stories with primary-source reporting. A good site to follow to actually stay current.

https://krebsonsecurity.com/ ↗

Practical Safety Tools

Have I Been Pwned

Troy Hunt's free breach-notification tool -- check whether your own email address has appeared in a known data breach. A hands-on, personally relevant way to see breach data instead of just reading about it.

https://haveibeenpwned.com/ ↗
Google Safety Center

Google's consumer-facing hub explaining account security settings, privacy controls, and built-in protections -- practical guidance you can actually apply to your own accounts today.

https://safety.google/ ↗

Certifications & Learning Paths

CompTIA Security+

The industry-standard entry-level security certification. Its exam objectives are essentially a well-structured syllabus of foundational cybersecurity topics, useful even if you're not taking the exam yet.

https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security ↗
SANS Cyber Aces

Free, selected training modules from the SANS Institute -- Linux, Windows, and networking security fundamentals -- made publicly available at no cost.

https://www.sans.org/cyberaces ↗
AP Cybersecurity (College Board)

The official course page for AP Cybersecurity, part of College Board's new AP Career Kickstart program -- developed with Cisco, launching nationally in fall 2026 with the first exam in May 2027. This is the actual course behind a lot of the content on this site.

https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-cybersecurity ↗

Hands-On Practice Platforms

TryHackMe

Browser-based, gamified hands-on security labs with guided beginner paths. No local setup required, which makes it a genuinely good first hands-on platform.

https://tryhackme.com/ ↗
OverTheWire: Bandit

A free, SSH-based wargame that teaches Linux and command-line fundamentals level by level. The classic starting point before attempting harder wargames.

https://overthewire.org/wargames/bandit/ ↗
picoCTF

Carnegie Mellon University's free, beginner-friendly capture-the-flag platform covering cryptography, web exploitation, forensics, and reverse engineering.

https://picoctf.org/ ↗
